# Decisions Log

Append-only record of the decisions you make and why. New entries go at the top. Never edit a past entry. When `/level-up` reaches Phase 2 and scopes a build, it writes the scoped spec here too, so the reasoning behind every build is captured the moment it is decided.

Per-entry format:

## YYYY-MM-DD: Short title

**Decision:** What you decided, in one line.

**Why:** The reason. This is the part future-you will need.

**Alternatives considered:** What you looked at and passed on.

**Owner:** Who owns the outcome.

---

Keep it terse. The why is the point. A decision with no reasoning is a note, not a decision.
