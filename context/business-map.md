# Business Map: The Seven Buckets

Your business, mapped across the seven buckets every business runs on (see `references/the-seven-buckets.md`). For each bucket: what lives in it, the tool that holds it, the one task that eats your time, what you wish the OS did, and how high a priority it is.

**Why this map matters.** It is the roadmap for your whole build, not a form you fill once. `/onboard` drafts it. Then it works for you:
- The **tool** names flow into `connections.md`, your Reach layer.
- The **time-sinks** and **wants** tell `/level-up` what to build next, in your words.
- The **priorities** tell `/audit` where the biggest gaps are.
- Every skill reads it to understand your business by domain.

A filled map turns "what should I even automate?" into a ranked to-do list.

> How to use it: start with the highest-priority bucket. Map it, wire its tool in `connections.md`, build the one thing you wished for, then move to the next. One bucket a week and you cover all seven inside two months.
