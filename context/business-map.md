Your business, mapped across the seven buckets every business runs on (see `references/the-seven-buckets.md`). This is the Memory-layer map: what lives in each bucket, the tool that holds it, and the one thing that eats your time there.

Fill it in plain words. `/onboard` can draft it from your intake. You sharpen it over time. The tool names here flow straight into `connections.md` when you wire each one.

> How to use it: start with the bucket marked highest pain. Map it, wire its tool in `connections.md`, then move to the next. One bucket a week.
