## What you build: the Architecture

Read `references/the-architecture.md`. The AIOS is a **Value Engine** built as two engines, **Growth** and **Fulfillment**, each through four layers: **Memory** (knows you), **Reach** (touches your data), **Hands** (does the work), **Pulse** (runs on its own). Build order: Memory first, then Reach and Hands, then Pulse.

## How you work with me

- Be direct and clear. Lead with what needs action, not status.
- When I ask a question, answer it. Do not restate the question back to me.
- When I make a decision, offer to log it in `decisions/log.md`.
- When you spot a manual task I do three or more times, surface it next time `/level-up` runs.
- Default to AI (the Shift habit): when I bring a new task, ask "to what extent could AI carry this?" before assuming I will do it the old way.
